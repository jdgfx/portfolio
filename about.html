Background

Josh Diaz is a Creative Director and Multimedia Producer with over 15 years of experience leading visual storytelling, motion design, and high-impact media production. Based out of Olney, MD, he specializes in building carefully curated visual systems, promotional campaigns, and brand experiences across broadcast, digital, and environmental mediums.

His career began in broadcast television—producing and designing for networks including MTV, NBC, and National Geographic—before transitioning into senior communications roles developing high-stakes strategic materials for high-ranking government and military leadership. As a creative lead, Josh has supervised teams of 10+ multidisciplinary creatives, guiding projects from concept through final delivery with an emphasis on rigorous standards and purpose-driven execution.

Josh’s creative philosophy centers on the principle that simplicity is the ultimate sophistication. Rather than relying on fleeting trends or visual gimmicks, he combines seamless editing, energetic motion graphics, and precise sound design to create sophisticated visual narratives that drive genuine engagement.
